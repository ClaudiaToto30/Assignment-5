The STL decomposition separates monthly U.S. retail sales into three components: trend, seasonality, and remainder. The trend shows an overall increase in sales, with a temporary decline around 2008–2009 and a sharp rise around 2020. Seasonality reveals recurring annual patterns, indicating that sales are influenced by seasonal factors. The remainder captures unexplained fluctuations, with larger deviations around 2020 and in later years. Overall, the decomposition highlights long-term sales growth, recurring seasonal patterns, and irregular variations that are useful for understanding historical sales and supporting future forecasts.
